Information We Collect We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when registering, expressing an interest in obtaining information about our services, or otherwise contacting us. CV work starts after the payment receipt is sent via WhatsApp and verified. If no job is found within two weeks from when the Ria receipt is received on WhatsApp, the €100 is refunded through Ria and the CV is provided free; job placement itself is free. • Process a separate €1,000 housing consultation fee first through Ria. The home search starts when its receipt arrives via WhatsApp. If no home is found within two weeks from then, the €1,000 is refunded through Ria. After approval, the stated initial rental payment is three months of rent as a deposit plus the first month of rent (four months total); it is paid separately and not through Ria. • Review rental documents and perform a credit check only after receiving the applicant’s written consent through WhatsApp. If approved, applicants are informed of the next steps. • Communicate with you regarding updates, service status, and customer support. 4.
